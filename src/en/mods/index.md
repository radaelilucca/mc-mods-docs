# Project catalog

This catalog grows as each project's documentation is reviewed and added.

## R-Ui Lib

A reusable client-side interface library for Minecraft mods.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge and NeoForge
- **For players:** installation, requirements, and library overview
- **For developers:** integration, component reference, themes, and development validation

[Open R-Ui Lib documentation](/en/mods/r-ui-lib/index.md)

## Fly by Instruments

Avionics sensors and technical CC:Tweaked integrations for Create Aeronautics.

- **Minecraft:** 1.21.1
- **Loader:** NeoForge
- **For players:** sensors, the Avionics Computer and its GPWS, the Modular Table, the Avionics Wire, and Computer Craft

[Open Fly by Instruments documentation](/en/mods/flight-safety-instruments/index.md)

## Hotbar Locker

Protect selected hotbar slots from automatic item replacement.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **For players:** installation, slot locks, and settings

[Open Hotbar Locker documentation](/en/mods/hotbar-locker/index.md)

## Sophisticated Backpacks: Digital Storage Expansion

Connect Sophisticated Backpacks upgrades to AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's Simple Storage, or SCC on NeoForge 1.21.1.

- **Minecraft:** 1.20.1 and 1.21.1
- **Loaders:** Forge for 1.20.1; NeoForge for 1.21.1
- **For players:** linking, filters, and a catalog of eight upgrades; SCC gameplay validation is pending

[Open Digital Storage Expansion documentation](/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/index.md)
