# Upgrade catalog

The mod adds eight upgrades for Sophisticated Backpacks. Each is a single universal item for its function: link it to AE2, Refined Storage, or Tom's Simple Storage; SCC 1.5.3 or newer is also supported on NeoForge 1.21.1. No separate item is needed for each network.

| Upgrade | What it does | Network support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remote Deposit** | Deposit matching items from the backpack into the linked network with its tab button. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Restock** | Retrieve matching items from the linked network into the backpack with its tab button. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Pickup** | Route newly collected items that match its filter to the backpack or network. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Magnet** | Collect nearby item drops that match its filter and route them to the backpack or network. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Refill** | Replenish configured player inventory slots from the backpack or network. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Feeding** | Feed the player carrying the backpack with configured food when hunger or injury conditions call for it. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Alchemy** | Automatically use configured consumables when their per-slot conditions are met. | Items: AE2, Refined Storage, Tom's; SCC on NeoForge |
| **Remote Pump** | Transfer configured fluids between the backpack tank or network and containers, nearby fluid handlers, or world fluids. | Fluids: AE2 and Refined Storage; SCC on NeoForge. Tom's is item-only. |

Use [Linking](linking) to connect an upgrade. See [Filters and source priority](filters) for allow/block filters, stored-item matching, and Backpack first versus Linked network first.

Full in-game validation of SCC gameplay is still pending.
