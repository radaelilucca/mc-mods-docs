# Flight Safety Instruments

Flight Safety Instruments adds avionics blocks for Create Aeronautics ships. Radar Altimeters and Terrain Scanners measure the world around a craft and expose readings through redstone and CC:Tweaked. The experimental avionics suite adds a Modular Table, FMS, cable network, and GPWS Computer.
