# Fly by Instruments

[CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/aeronautics-fly-by-instruments)

Fly by Instruments adds avionics blocks for Create Aeronautics ships. Radar Altimeters and Terrain Scanners measure the world around a craft and expose readings through redstone and CC:Tweaked. The experimental avionics suite adds a Modular Table, FMS, cable network, and GPWS Computer.

**Documentation reflects Fly by Instruments 0.1.0** · Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge.
